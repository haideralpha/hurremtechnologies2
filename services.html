Where AI Stops, People Start

AI is powerful, but it can't do everything. The best results come from AI and skilled people working together. That's how we build every solution.

AI for speed. People for judgment. You get both.

How the work splits

  • Teaching: AI handles scheduling and reminders; teachers deliver live classes.
  • Chatbots: AI answers routine questions; a VA steps in when a person is needed.
  • Customer care: AI routes requests; our team handles sensitive conversations.
  • Decisions: AI prepares the work; people approve it.
