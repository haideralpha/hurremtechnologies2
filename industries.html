Who we help

Industries
We Serve

Specialized support for the industries where operations and reputation matter most. In each one, AI takes the routine work and our people handle what needs judgment.

Education

For online academies, tutoring businesses, and ESL and language-teaching programs.

  • Live online classes with remote teachers, including English language teaching
  • Class scheduling, reminders and rescheduling
  • Student progress tracking and parent updates
  • Enrollment inquiries and admin support

AI + people: AI handles scheduling, reminders and progress reports; teachers deliver live classes.

Healthcare

Administrative support for practices and clinics, so staff spend less time on the phone and paperwork. We don't provide clinical services.

  • Appointment scheduling and reminders
  • Patient communication support for non-clinical questions
  • Front-desk admin and inbox management
  • Practice reviews and online listings

AI + people: AI sends reminders and answers routine questions; your remote admin team handles calls and anything sensitive.

Real Estate

For property managers, leasing teams and investors. Our team works in common property-management platforms such as AppFolio, Buildium and Yardi.

  • Tenant communication and maintenance request coordination
  • Leasing inquiries, showings and follow-up
  • Vendor scheduling and lease renewal paperwork
  • Listing creation and updates

AI + people: AI answers first inquiries and logs requests; your VA coordinates vendors, tenants and showings.

Mining & Natural Resources

Back-office support for operators, traders and exporters who handle heavy documentation and international buyers.

  • Document and report management
  • Buyer and export correspondence support
  • Data entry and records
  • Lead research for B2B buyers

AI + people: AI organizes documents and drafts routine reports; our team manages buyer communication and checks the records.

Construction & Home Services

For contractors and trades who lose jobs when calls go unanswered while they're on site.

  • Missed-call text-back and lead follow-up
  • Job scheduling and customer updates
  • Review requests after every completed job
  • Estimates, invoices and admin support

AI + people: AI replies to missed calls and sends review requests; your VA books jobs and handles customers.

Professional Services

For law firms, consultancies and agencies that want less admin and faster client response.

  • Client intake and appointment booking
  • Calendar and inbox management
  • Document formatting and data entry
  • Client follow-up and reviews

AI + people: AI handles intake forms and reminders; your assistant manages clients and keeps work on track.
