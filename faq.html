Reputation Shield

Review Management Questions

It depends on the platform and the nature of the review. Google typically processes removal requests within 5–15 business days. Yelp takes 7–21 days. For reviews that clearly violate platform policies (fake accounts, spam, defamatory content), our removal rate is significantly higher than DIY attempts — because we know exactly what documentation and arguments each platform requires. We'll give you a realistic assessment during your free audit.
We monitor and manage reviews across 40+ platforms, including: Google Business Profile, Yelp, Facebook, Trustpilot, Better Business Bureau (BBB), Glassdoor, Indeed, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Avvo (legal), Houzz (construction), and all major industry-specific review sites. We also monitor news articles, forum posts, and social media mentions that could affect your reputation.
No one can guarantee review removal — any company that does is misleading you. Review platforms have ultimate authority over what stays up. What we guarantee is a thorough, professional process that maximizes your chances of removal for reviews that violate platform policies. For reviews that can't be removed, we focus on generating positive reviews to dilute their impact and crafting professional responses that minimize reputational damage.
